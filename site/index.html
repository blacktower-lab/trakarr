trakarr Github

Keep your
ratio guarded!

trakarr holds downloads when your ratio drops and releases them once seeding brings it back.

Install on Debian or Ubuntu

bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/blacktower-lab/trakarr/main/scripts/install.sh)"

How it works

  1. Watch

    trakarr follows qBittorrent and works out each tracker's ratio from what its torrents have uploaded and downloaded.

  2. Hold

    Below your hold line, that tracker's downloads are stopped or throttled, and seeding carries on.

  3. Release

    Seeding brings the ratio back up, and past your release line the downloads go on.
